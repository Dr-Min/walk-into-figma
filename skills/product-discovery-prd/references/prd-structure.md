# PRD Structure

## Contents

1. Document control
2. Product definition
3. Users and journeys
4. Requirements
5. Commercial and operational policy
6. Delivery and validation

## 1. Document control

- title, product name, codename;
- owner and stakeholders;
- version, date, status;
- source list and precedence;
- approval history;
- decision log link.

## 2. Product definition

- purpose and background;
- problem statement and evidence;
- goals, measurable outcomes, and non-goals;
- target users, exclusions, personas, and jobs;
- value proposition and experience principles;
- competitive context without unsupported claims.

## 3. Users and journeys

- primary and secondary journeys;
- acquisition, onboarding, activation, retention, monetization, cancellation;
- happy path and recovery path;
- cross-platform differences;
- accessibility and localization expectations.

## 4. Requirements

- IA and capability inventory;
- functional requirements by domain;
- roles and permissions;
- data lifecycle;
- states, errors, retries, fallbacks, and service degradation;
- performance, availability, security, accessibility, and compatibility;
- acceptance criteria for each MVP capability.

## 5. Commercial and operational policy

- plans, prices, currency, tax handling, entitlements, quotas;
- renewal, expiration, upgrade, downgrade, cancellation, refunds;
- metering, credits, priority of consumption, restoration;
- moderation, reporting, blocking, appeals, and administrator tools;
- privacy, retention, deletion, model-training use, regional controls;
- vendor dependencies, failover, and cost limits;
- unit economics assumptions versus actual net profit.

## 6. Delivery and validation

- MVP, later releases, explicit exclusions;
- analytics events, KPIs, guardrails, experiment design;
- launch gates, migration, rollout, rollback, and support;
- risks, sensitivities, dependencies, and owners;
- final unresolved-decision register;
- approval record.

Adapt the structure to the product. Mark a non-applicable section `N/A` with a reason; do not silently omit it.
